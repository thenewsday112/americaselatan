Venezuela names Oliver Blanco, a former opposition aide, as foreign minister

📅 Oct 30, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

Venezuela’s acting President Delcy Rodríguez has appointed Oliver Blanco, a 35-year-old diplomat who began his political career alongside a veteran opposition leader, as foreign minister in a reshuffle of her government’s international team. Blanco replaces Félix Plasencia, whom Rodríguez has tasked with moving to the United Kingdom to recover Venezuelan gold held at the Bank of England.

Pressure mounts on Paraguay’s Central Bank over doubts about some lenders’ solvency

📅 Oct 18, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

The Association of Banks of Paraguay, which groups 14 private lenders, on Sunday asked the Central Bank of Paraguay to report on the solvency of institutions that received facilities in merger processes, days after senators from the ruling party and the opposition called on President Santiago Peña to remove the institution’s leadership.

Lubetkin warns Flávio Bolsonaro that “it is not easy to leave Mercosur”

IMF trims Latin America’s outlook to 2.3% and sharply cuts Argentina’s

📅 Oct 13, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

The International Monetary Fund has lowered its 2026 growth forecast for Latin America and the Caribbean to 2.3%, a tenth of a point less than in July, and sharply cut Argentina’s, from 3.5% to 2.1%. Chile and Uruguay are also among the region’s largest downward revisions, according to the World Economic Outlook presented on Monday in Bangkok, during the annual meetings of the Fund and the World Bank.

The US waives beef tariffs for Brazil and Paraguay, but not for Argentina and Uruguay

📅 Aug 28, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

US President Donald Trump signed a proclamation on August 26 temporarily waiving tariffs on imports of up to 300,000 tonnes of lean beef trimmings, a measure that excludes countries already holding their own quotas in the US market, among them Argentina and Uruguay. The main beneficiaries will be Brazil and Paraguay.

Uruguay issues Swiss franc bonds for the first time

📅 Jun 27, 2025  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

Uruguay issued sovereign bonds in Swiss francs for the first time Thursday, totaling 320 million (about US$400 million) to help finance a US$6 billion fiscal deficit in 2025. The bonds, with five- and ten-year terms, carry an average annual interest rate of 1.33%. Economy Minister Gabriel Oddone highlighted the favorable interest rates and noted that Uruguay was the only Latin American country currently issuing bonds in this currency, tapping into a market of high-quality investors.
